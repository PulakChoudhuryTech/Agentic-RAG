# Parental Leave Policy

**Category:** HR | **Applies to:** All full-time employees | **Effective date:** 2025-01-01

## Overview

Acme Global provides paid parental leave to eligible full-time employees for the
birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. This policy is designed to give
new parents time to bond with their child while providing job protection and
continued benefits coverage during the leave period.

Parental leave is available to all parents regardless of gender, and applies
equally to birthing and non-birthing parents, adoptive parents, and foster
parents. Leave entitlements vary by country to comply with local labor law, as
detailed below.

## Eligibility

To be eligible for paid parental leave, an employee must:

- Be classified as a full-time employee (30+ hours/week)
- Have completed at least 6 months of continuous employment (tenure_months >= 6)
  as of the expected leave start date
- Provide at least 30 days advance notice where the leave is planned (birth or
  adoption placement dates), except in cases of premature birth or emergency
  placement

Part-time and contract employees are not eligible for paid parental leave under
this policy, but may be eligible for unpaid leave under applicable local law.

## Leave Duration by Country

### United States

- 12 weeks of fully paid parental leave for the primary caregiver
- 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave for the secondary caregiver
- Leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth/adoption date
- Can be taken continuously or split into two blocks with manager approval

### India

- 26 weeks of fully paid maternity leave for birthing employees, in line with
  the Maternity Benefit (Amendment) Act, 2017
- 2 weeks of fully paid paternity leave for non-birthing parents (spouse of the
  birthing parent), to be taken within 3 months of the birth
- 12 weeks of fully paid adoption leave for the adopting parent, applicable when
  the adopted child is below 3 months of age at the time of adoption
- Employees in India remain eligible for the full 26 weeks of maternity leave
  regardless of the number of previous children, in accordance with company
  policy exceeding the statutory minimum for the first two children
- Crèche (childcare) benefit: employees with children under 6 years old may
  claim reimbursement for creche facilities as part of the return-to-work
  support program

### United Kingdom

- 90% of salary for the first 6 weeks, then statutory parental pay for up to 33
  additional weeks, for the primary caregiver
- 2 weeks of fully paid paternity leave for the secondary caregiver

### Germany

- Following statutory Elternzeit provisions; employees may take up to 12 months
  of leave with Elterngeld wage-replacement benefit coordinated through the
  local HR team

## Requesting Leave

1. Notify your manager and HR Business Partner as early as possible.
2. Submit a parental leave request through the Workday self-service portal
   under "Time Off > Leave of Absence".
3. HR will confirm your eligibility, country-specific entitlement, and expected
   return date within 5 business days.
4. Benefits (health insurance, retirement contributions) continue uninterrupted
   during paid parental leave.

## Eligibility Based on Employee Profile

Because eligibility depends on employment type, tenure, and country, HR
recommends checking your current profile (employment type, country, and
tenure) in Workday before submitting a request. Employees with less than 6
months of tenure should speak with their HR Business Partner about unpaid
leave alternatives available under local law.
