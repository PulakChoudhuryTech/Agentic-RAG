# PTO and Vacation Policy

**Category:** HR | **Applies to:** All full-time employees | **Effective date:** 2025-01-01

## Overview

Acme Global provides paid time off (PTO) to support employee well-being and
work-life balance. This document covers vacation day accrual, carryover rules,
and how to request time off.

## Accrual

Full-time employees accrue vacation days based on tenure:

| Tenure              | Annual vacation days |
|----------------------|----------------------|
| 0-2 years             | 15 days              |
| 2-5 years              | 20 days              |
| 5+ years               | 25 days              |

Vacation days accrue monthly (annual entitlement / 12) starting from the
employee's hire date. Newly hired employees begin accruing from month one but
may not take vacation until they have accrued a positive balance.

Your current vacation day balance (accrued minus used) is tracked in Workday
and can be viewed under "Time Off > My Balances". This is the authoritative,
real-time source for how many vacation days you currently have available --
this document only describes the accrual policy, not your personal balance.

## Carryover

Employees may carry over up to 5 unused vacation days into the following
calendar year. Any balance above 5 days as of December 31 is forfeited unless
local law requires otherwise (e.g., several European countries mandate full
carryover in specific circumstances -- check with your local HR Business
Partner).

## Requesting Time Off

1. Submit a time-off request in Workday at least 2 weeks in advance for
   requests longer than 3 consecutive days.
2. Your manager will approve or deny the request within 3 business days.
3. Approved time off is automatically deducted from your vacation balance.

## Sick Leave

Sick leave is tracked separately from vacation and does not draw down your
vacation balance. Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year, which do not
carry over.

## Public Holidays

Public holidays follow the employee's local country calendar and are
published each January by the regional HR team. Public holidays do not count
against vacation balance.

## Unlimited PTO (Applicable Roles Only)

Employees at Director level and above are on an "unlimited PTO" policy: there
is no fixed annual entitlement, but time off still requires manager approval
and should be used responsibly, targeting a minimum of 15 days per year.
