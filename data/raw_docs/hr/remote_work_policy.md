# Remote Work Policy

**Category:** HR | **Applies to:** All employees | **Effective date:** 2025-01-01

## Overview

Acme Global operates a hybrid-first work model. This policy describes
eligibility for remote work, home office expectations, and equipment support.

## Work Location Categories

- **Hybrid (default)**: 3 days/week in the office, 2 days remote, subject to
  team-specific schedules set by managers.
- **Fully remote**: Approved on a role-by-role basis for positions without a
  business need for regular office presence. Requires VP-level sign-off.
- **Fully in-office**: Required for roles handling regulated physical
  materials or requiring specialized on-site equipment.

## Equipment

Employees working remotely are provided a company laptop and a one-time $300
home office stipend for a monitor, chair, or desk. Requests for additional
hardware go through the IT Hardware Request process (see the IT documents for
details).

## Working Across Time Zones

Employees working remotely from a different time zone than their team must
maintain at least 4 hours of overlap with their core team's working hours,
unless otherwise agreed with their manager.

## International Remote Work

Working remotely from a country other than your employment country requires
prior approval from HR and Legal due to tax and employment law implications,
and is generally limited to 30 days per year without a formal relocation.

## Security Requirements

All remote work must be performed over the company VPN when accessing
internal systems (HR data, source code repositories, financial systems). See
the VPN Troubleshooting guide in the IT documents if you have connectivity
issues.
