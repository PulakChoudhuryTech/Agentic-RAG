# Employee Benefits Overview

**Category:** HR | **Applies to:** All full-time employees | **Effective date:** 2025-01-01

## Health Insurance

Acme Global offers three health plan tiers: Basic, Standard, and Premium. All
full-time employees are automatically enrolled in the Standard plan on their
first day, with a 30-day window to switch tiers or add dependents through
Workday's "Benefits" tab.

- **Basic**: Company covers 70% of premium, employee pays 30%.
- **Standard**: Company covers 85% of premium, employee pays 15%.
- **Premium**: Company covers 90% of premium, employee pays 10%; includes
  vision and dental.

Dependents (spouse/partner and children under 26) can be added during
enrollment or within 30 days of a qualifying life event (marriage, birth,
adoption).

## Retirement Contributions

Acme Global matches employee retirement contributions (401(k) in the US,
equivalent local pension schemes elsewhere) up to 6% of base salary, vested
immediately with no waiting period.

## Wellness Stipend

Employees receive an annual $500 wellness stipend reimbursable for gym
memberships, fitness classes, or mental health app subscriptions. Submit
receipts via the Expense portal under category "Wellness".

## Life and Disability Insurance

Basic life insurance (1x annual salary) and short-term disability coverage are
provided at no cost to all full-time employees. Supplemental life insurance
can be purchased at group rates.

## Benefits During Leave

Health insurance and retirement matching continue uninterrupted during paid
parental leave and approved medical leave. See the Parental Leave Policy for
country-specific leave durations.

## Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

All employees and their household members have access to free, confidential
counseling (up to 6 sessions per issue per year) through the EAP, available
24/7 via the number listed on the Benefits portal.
