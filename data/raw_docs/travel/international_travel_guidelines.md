# International Travel Guidelines

**Category:** Travel | **Applies to:** All employees | **Effective date:** 2025-01-01

## Visa and Entry Requirements

Employees are responsible for confirming their own visa/entry requirements
for their destination country, but the corporate travel portal will flag
likely visa needs at time of booking based on your passport nationality.
Visa application fees for approved business trips are reimbursable; expedited
processing fees require manager pre-approval.

## Health and Safety

- All international business travelers are automatically covered by the
  company's global travel medical insurance (details and the 24/7
  emergency assistance number are emailed automatically after booking).
- Check the destination's entry on the Travel Risk Dashboard
  (travel.acmeglobal.com/risk) before booking; countries flagged "High Risk"
  require additional Security team sign-off.
- Register your itinerary with the company's travel tracking system
  (automatic when booked through the portal) so the Security team can reach
  you in an emergency.

## Data Security While Traveling

- Use a loaner "travel laptop" (available via IT hardware request) instead of
  your primary laptop when traveling to countries on the Restricted
  Destinations list, to limit data exposure.
- Always connect to company systems via VPN, even on hotel or conference
  Wi-Fi. See the VPN Troubleshooting guide for connectivity issues.
- Report a lost or stolen device immediately, both to local authorities and
  via an urgent IT ticket (see Laptop and Hardware Request Process).

## Working While Traveling for Business (Not Remote Work)

Business travel is distinct from the International Remote Work provisions in
the Remote Work Policy. Business travel does not require HR/Legal approval
for durations under 2 weeks when travel is for a specific business purpose
(client meeting, conference, training) rather than working remotely from a
personal-choice location.

## Currency and Payment

Use your corporate card for all trip expenses where accepted. If a corporate
card is not available or not accepted, pay personally and submit for
reimbursement in your local currency; the Expense portal converts using the
exchange rate on the transaction date.
