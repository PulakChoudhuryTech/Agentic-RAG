# Travel Expense Policy

**Category:** Travel | **Applies to:** All employees | **Effective date:** 2025-01-01

## Overview

This policy covers reimbursable expenses for approved business travel,
booking guidelines, and submission requirements.

## Booking Travel

All flights and hotels for business travel must be booked through the
corporate travel portal (travel.acmeglobal.com) to ensure duty-of-care
tracking and negotiated corporate rates. Booking outside the portal requires
pre-approval from your manager and may not be reimbursed at full cost.

- **Flights**: Economy class for flights under 6 hours; Premium Economy or
  Business class permitted for flights over 6 hours with manager approval.
- **Hotels**: Up to a nightly cap that varies by city cost-of-living tier
  (Tier 1 cities like New York, London, Bangalore: $280/night; Tier 2:
  $180/night; Tier 3: $120/night).

## Reimbursable Expenses

- Ground transportation (taxi, rideshare, rental car) to/from airports and
  business meetings
- Meals while traveling, up to $75/day domestic, $100/day international, with
  itemized receipts required for any single expense over $25
- Reasonable baggage fees for business-necessary luggage
- Wi-Fi charges while traveling for business purposes

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

- Alcohol (except at approved client entertainment events, capped separately)
- Personal entertainment, spa services, minibar charges
- Travel insurance beyond the company's standard corporate policy (already
  included automatically for all business trips)
- Upgrades not pre-approved by a manager

## Submitting Expenses

Submit expense reports within 15 days of trip completion through the Expense
portal, with itemized receipts attached for every line item over $25.
Reports submitted more than 60 days after trip completion require additional
Finance approval and may be denied.

## Per Diem Alternative

For trips where itemizing meals is impractical (e.g., extended international
travel), employees may elect a per diem rate instead of itemized meal
reimbursement, set by destination country and available in the Expense
portal's per diem calculator.
