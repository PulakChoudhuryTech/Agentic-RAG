# Password Reset Policy

**Category:** IT | **Applies to:** All employees | **Effective date:** 2025-01-01

## Password Requirements

Corporate passwords must be at least 14 characters, include at least one
number and one symbol, and cannot reuse any of your last 10 passwords.
Passwords expire every 180 days.

## Self-Service Reset

Most employees can reset their own password without a ticket:

1. Go to `password.acmeglobal.com` from any device with internet access.
2. Enter your corporate email address.
3. Complete MFA verification (SMS code or authenticator app).
4. Choose a new password meeting the requirements above.

Self-service reset takes effect within 2 minutes. If you're on VPN when you
reset your password, you will need to disconnect and reconnect to the VPN
with your new password.

## When Self-Service Isn't Available

If you can't complete MFA verification (e.g., lost phone, new device not yet
registered), self-service reset is not available and you must contact the IT
Service Desk directly (not via ticket, since you may be locked out of the
ticketing portal too) at extension 4357 (HELP) for identity verification and
a manual reset.

## Account Lockout

After 5 failed login attempts, accounts are locked for 30 minutes
automatically. Repeated lockouts should be reported via an IT ticket
categorized "Access > Account Lockout" in case of a compromised credential
investigation.
