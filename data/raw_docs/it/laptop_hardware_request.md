# Laptop and Hardware Request Process

**Category:** IT | **Applies to:** All employees | **Effective date:** 2025-01-01

## Standard Issue Equipment

New employees receive a standard-issue laptop (14" MacBook Pro or equivalent
Windows ThinkPad, employee's choice) configured with company security
software before their start date.

## Requesting a Replacement or Upgrade

Hardware requests (replacement laptop, additional monitor, keyboard/mouse,
docking station) are submitted as an IT ticket categorized "Hardware >
Request" including:

- The specific item requested
- Business justification (e.g., "current laptop battery no longer holds a
  charge", "role change requires dual monitors")
- Manager approval (attached or referenced in the ticket)

Standard hardware requests are fulfilled within 5-7 business days. Urgent
replacements (e.g., a dead laptop blocking work) should be marked "High"
urgency and are typically fulfilled within 1-2 business days via IT's loaner
pool while the replacement ships.

## Loss or Theft

Lost or stolen equipment must be reported immediately via an IT ticket
categorized "Hardware > Loss/Theft" so IT can remotely lock and wipe the
device. This is time-sensitive for security reasons and should not wait for
standard ticket queue processing -- also call the IT Service Desk hotline
directly.

## Returning Equipment

Equipment must be returned to IT within 5 business days of employment end
date or role change to non-laptop-eligible status, via the prepaid shipping
label provided by HR during offboarding.
