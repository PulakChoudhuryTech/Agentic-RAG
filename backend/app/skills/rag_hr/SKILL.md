## Answer guidance

HR topics are often eligibility-dependent (tenure, country, employment
type). If the retrieved context ties an answer to one of those factors, say
so explicitly rather than giving a blanket answer -- an employee acting on
an incomplete HR answer can have real consequences (missing a leave window,
under-claiming a benefit).
